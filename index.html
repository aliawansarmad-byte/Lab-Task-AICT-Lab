About the Faculty

The Faculty of Computing and Artificial Intelligence provides education in modern computing technologies, software development, and intelligent systems.

Students learn programming, databases, networking, artificial intelligence, and software engineering. Practical learning helps students develop problem-solving and technical skills.

Our goal is to prepare students for the challenges of the modern technology industry. Knowledge, innovation, and creativity are important parts of computing education.

Students are encouraged to learn HTML, programming, and other modern technologies.

Academic Programs

The faculty offers the following academic programs:

  • Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence (BSAI)
  • Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering (BSSE)
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS)
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT)

Importance of Computing Education

Computing education develops logical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving abilities. It helps students understand how software, computer systems, and artificial intelligence work.

Learning HTML is an important first step in web development because it provides the structure of a webpage.
